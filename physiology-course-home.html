How to thrive here

Three habits that make this course work for you.

01

Keep the pace

This course is online and self-paced within each week. A little each day beats cramming. Follow the weekly schedule and you stay ahead.

02

Reason, don't memorize

Physiology is mechanism. Ask why at every step. Understanding how a system works transfers to the exam and the clinic. Memorized lists fade.

03

Do the labs by hand

Each week's dry lab is worked by hand: calculations, graphs, and predictions from real data. Working it by hand is what makes it stick.

Questions, answered

What students usually want to know first.

What should I do first?+

Read the syllabus, open Week 1, and work the first dry lab. That is all you need to start strong.

It's online. When do I work?+

The course is online and you work at your own pace within each week. There are no live meetings. Follow the weekly schedule and meet the deadlines, and you can work at the times that fit your life.

How do exams work?+

Three unit exams fall in Weeks 4, 7, and 11, and a cumulative final closes the term. Your weekly dry labs and short retrieval checks count too. Full weights and dates are in your syllabus.

Do the labs really have to be by hand?+

Yes. The dry labs are worked by hand: calculations, plotted graphs, and written predictions from data. Hand work is the integrity mechanism for this course. Photograph or scan your pages and upload them.

Where do I get help when I'm stuck?+

Start with the weekly unit and the schedule. Office hours and contact details are in your syllabus. You are never stuck alone in this course.

Need help · Grade recovery

Falling behind? You can turn it around.

Struggling in physiology is almost always about how you study, not whether you can do it. The fix is a study system, built step by step, and this course hands you one.

  • Retrieve, don't reread. Practice pulling answers from memory instead of rereading notes.
  • Space it out. Short sessions across several days beat one long cram.
  • Draw the mechanism. Redraw the pathways and graphs from memory until they stick.
