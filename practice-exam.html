    How to use this all term

    Retrieve, diagnose, study, retrieve again.

    RetrieveDiagnoseStudyRetrieve againCompareAdjust

    Take an exam before you study, not after. The Gap Finder tells you where the holes are, so you study those competencies instead of rereading everything. Then take another exam on the same weeks and compare. The number that matters is the change between attempts, not the first score.

    Two different things leave this page. The weekly practice log is a PDF you upload in Canvas for me alone. It shows your attempts and scores by week so I can reach out before an exam if it looks like you could use help. Your discussion post is public, and it never needs a score. Say whether you are improving (a lot, a little, not yet), how many attempts you made, what you changed about how you study, and whether the change helped. If you want to quote your change in percentage points, you can, but you do not have to.

    The results report you save at the end lists the competencies you missed, never the questions. That is on purpose. Go back to the note sheet and the recall cards for those competencies and rebuild the idea, then come back and test it.
