02
Graded · Note sheet, part 1 of 2

Note sheet, first pass

Part 1 of one assignment. By hand, on paper, in your first color. Do not open the videos yet.

This pass is your textbook and your own notes, nothing else. Work through the sheet one box at a time and organize what you can already put together: mini mind maps, lists, sequences, arrows. Keep it short, and stop while half of every box is still empty, because the second pass goes in the same box.

Doing this before you watch is the whole point. You are supposed to arrive at the videos already holding a partly built picture, so that watching them fills gaps you can feel rather than washing over you.

Do not turn anything in yet. Both passes go on the same sheet, and you submit it once, at step 5. If you hand it in here you will be turning in a half finished sheet in a single color, which is exactly the thing that does not count. Keep going to step 3, then come back to the same sheet at step 5. Steps 2 and 5 are one assignment worth 20 percent, not two.

05
Graded · Note sheet, part 2 of 2

Note sheet, second pass

Part 2 of the same assignment. Same sheet you started at step 2, second color, nothing erased.

Now that you have watched, go back through the sheet in a second color and add what you were missing, correct what you had wrong, and write down whatever finally made sense once you heard it explained. Leave the first pass visible underneath, mistakes included. Those are the most useful marks on the page.

Why two colors. When I read your sheet I can see exactly which parts came from your reading and which came from the videos. A sheet that arrives in one color tells me the videos never got watched, and that sheet is not finished, whatever is written on it. This is also the honest way to find out what you actually understood on your own, which is the thing your midterm asks about.

Now you submit. This is the only time you turn the note sheet in, with both passes on it, both colors showing. Photograph it in color, in good light, held flat.
