Per sheet of paper

Before you start

  1. Pick two pens you can tell apart. Pass one is the book, before any video. Pass two is a second color, after the videos, into the same boxes.
  2. Stop pass one while half of every box is still empty. That empty half is the point.
  3. The box is a drawing. Read your prompt first, then divide the box to fit it. Some prompts want a picture with labels running out of it. Some want two panels side by side. Some want a row of small graphs. The division is yours to make.
  4. Words are allowed inside the drawing. A sentence running the width of the box is not. If a box reads like a paragraph, redo it as a word picture.

About the paper

You do not have to print this. Two per competency page is what it opens on, and three fits a whole week onto a few sheets. Printing at home, at work, or in the library all count. So does not printing.

If you are not printing, rule one box per competency onto your own paper. The template below shows a finished one. A hand ruled sheet is graded exactly the same as a printed one.

Working on a tablet counts too. Import the PDF into whatever you draw in and use two pen colors.

What to copy from this. Not the layout, the habit. A picture rather than a paragraph. Short labels on lines that run out to that picture. Small boxes joined by arrows when something happens in an order. And somewhere on the page, the thing broken on purpose, with the arrows followed to what stops. Two pen colors throughout, both passes into the same box.

Turning it in

Photograph every sheet in order, in good light, flat on the table. Both colors have to be readable. Combine them into one PDF and upload that to the week’s Canvas assignment. Printed and hand ruled sheets are graded the same way.

Full submission and grading detail is on the note sheet assignment page.
