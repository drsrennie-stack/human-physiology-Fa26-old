Per sheet of paper

Before you start

  1. Pick two pens you can tell apart. Pass one is the book, before any video. Pass two is a second colour, after the videos, into the same boxes.
  2. Stop pass one while half of every box is still empty. That empty half is the point.
  3. All four boxes are drawings. Not just box 1. Box 2 is labels with lines out to the picture. Box 3 is little boxes with arrows between them in the order things happen. Box 4 follows those arrows to whatever stops.
  4. Words are allowed inside the drawing. A sentence running the width of the box is not. If a box reads like a paragraph, redo it as a word picture.

About the paper

You do not have to print this. Two per competency page is what it opens on, and three fits a whole week onto a few sheets. Printing at home, at work, or in the library all count. So does not printing.

If you are not printing, rule the four boxes onto your own paper. The template below shows the layout and what goes in each box, drawn out. A hand ruled sheet is graded exactly the same as a printed one.

Working on a tablet counts too. Import the PDF into whatever you draw in and use two pen colours.

Not printing? Rule this onto your own paper.

The four boxes are the same wherever you draw them. One competency to a sheet, or fold a page in half and do two. This example is anatomy, from BIO 004, on purpose. Nothing in it goes on your physiology sheet, so you are looking at how a box gets built and not at answers to copy.

What to copy from this. The four boxes and their labels, and the habit inside them. Box 1 is a picture. Box 2 is short labels on lines that run out to that picture. Box 3 is small boxes joined by arrows in the order things happen. Box 4 takes those same boxes, crosses out the one that failed, and follows the arrows to what stops. Two pen colours throughout, both passes into the same boxes.

Turning it in

Photograph every sheet in order, in good light, flat on the table. Both colours have to be readable. Combine them into one PDF and upload that to the week’s Canvas assignment. Printed and hand ruled sheets are graded the same way.
