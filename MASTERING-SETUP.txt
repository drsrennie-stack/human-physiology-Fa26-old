MASTERING SETUP NOTES  (instructor only, not on any student page)
=================================================================
From your build sheet, kept here so it does not get lost:

- Assign activities BY NAME from the Item Library, not by number.
  Pearson renumbers occasionally.
- Each activity has an assignable pre-lab and post-lab quiz. The credit
  model grades these mostly on completion, with mastery verified on the
  midterm teach-backs instead.
- One assignment per week, named plainly, e.g. "Week 03 Lab · Membranes",
  so the Mastering list reads in order.
- All 12 exercises get used across the term. Ex 4, 8, 10 and 12 are
  deliberately split across two weeks each, where their activities belong
  physiologically.

Every week opens Monday and closes Sunday 11:59 pm, except Week 1 which
opens Tuesday September 8, and Week 15 which closes Wednesday December 16
with the term.

WHERE THE DATA LIVES NOW: the LABS array at the bottom of
assignment-physioex.html, and a short EX list in course-start.html so the
week entry names the exercise. Change a week in both if you move one.
