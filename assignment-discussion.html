What this is

One post, not two. Each week your post does two jobs at the same time: it works through something from Week 1, and it says something honest about your own thinking on that same material. Not a content post and a separate reflection post. One post that carries both.

Two deadlines

Your post is due before your replies

Your post: Friday at 10:00 pm. Earlier in the week than everything else, on purpose. Your classmates cannot reply to a post that is not there yet, so a late post takes the week away from someone else as well as from you.

Your two replies: Sunday at 10:00 pm, the same deadline as the rest of the week's work. That gives you the weekend to read what everyone else worked through.

Both parts follow the usual rule: up to 24 hours late is an automatic 50 percent reduction, and after 24 hours it is a zero.

What goes in your post

  1. Pick one thing from this weekA mechanism, a loop, a calculation, a result from your lab. One thing, worked properly, beats a survey of everything.
  2. Work through itExplain it the way you would to a classmate who missed the week. Use the real terminology, and say why each step follows from the one before.
  3. Then turn the lens aroundThis is the half people skip. Where was your prediction wrong this week, and what were you assuming that made it look right? What did you have to go back and re-learn? Which part still feels shaky when you try to explain it without looking?

This week’s discussion

Replying to two classmates

A reply that counts

You add a mechanism the post left out. You catch something that is not quite right and explain why. You connect it to something from an earlier week, or you ask a question you genuinely want answered.

A reply that does not

You say you agree. You say it was helpful. You restate what the post already said, or you compliment it without touching the physiology in it.

Using AI on this

AI is closed on discussions, including your replies. The whole value of this assignment is an accurate account of what your reasoning actually looked like this week, and that is not something a model can report on your behalf. Write it yourself, in your own words, including the parts where you were wrong.
