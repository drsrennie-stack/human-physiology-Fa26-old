HOW TO USE
  Canvas page > Edit > the </> HTML editor button > paste > Save.
  Paste into the HTML editor, not the rich text box, or Canvas
  shows you the code instead of the page.

HOW EVERYTHING IS NAMED
  Every assignment in this course is "Week N <thing>". The Learning
  Labs are no exception: Week 2 Learning Lab, Week 3 Learning Lab,
  and so on. There is deliberately NO "Learning Lab 1, 2, 3, 4"
  numbering, because that runs one behind the week and gives
  students a second index to keep straight.

  Suggested Canvas titles:
    Week 2 Learning Lab: Reading a Physiology Figure or Graph
    Week 2 Discussion: Reading a Physiology Figure or Graph
    Week 3 Learning Lab: Previewing an Unfamiliar Chapter
    Week 3 Discussion: Previewing an Unfamiliar Chapter
    Week 4 Learning Lab: Spaced Recall and the Gap Finder
    Week 4 Discussion: Spaced Recall and the Gap Finder
    Week 5 Learning Lab: Auditing AI on Cell Transport
    Week 5 Discussion: Auditing AI on Cell Transport

ABOUT THE HEIGHTS
  Every number below was measured on the rendered page at 820px
  wide, the usual Canvas content column, then rounded up. Nothing
  here is a guess.

  Where a page grows as a student works through it, the height is
  the FULLY OPEN one. The Learning Labs nearly double, so a height
  taken from the first screen would cut the discussion off at the
  exact moment they earned it.

  On a phone every page is taller, because it stacks into one
  column. The frame scrolls internally there. Nothing is lost.

  THE NOTE SHEET IS THE EXCEPTION. It runs 5,850px in a light week
  to 20,900px in Week 5, because it is a printable sheet with one
  block per competency. Do not embed it. Link it, or attach the PDF
  from sheets/. Its snippet is at the bottom if you want it anyway.
