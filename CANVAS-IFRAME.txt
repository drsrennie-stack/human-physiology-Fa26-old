IF YOU CHANGE A HEIGHT AND NOTHING HAPPENS

The height appears ONCE now, in height="...". Change that number and the
box changes.

Earlier versions of these snippets also carried min-height in the style
attribute, with the same number in it. Editing only one of the two did
nothing visible, because min-height held the box open at the old value.
If you have a snippet already pasted into Canvas that will not resize,
look for min-height in its style attribute and delete it.

Too much white space under the content means the height is larger than
the page. Too little means the iframe is scrolling internally. Adjust
in steps of about 100 and reload.
