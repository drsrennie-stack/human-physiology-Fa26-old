Five ways to study

All of them make you produce the answer instead of recognizing it. None of them are graded. Rotate between them; the switching is part of what makes it stick.

  • Practice exam

    A real exam over any weeks you pick, the competencies to work on, and your weekly log.

    Log is required weekly

  • Recall cards

    Spaced recall, with the full reason every answer is right or wrong.

  • Draw it

    A blank canvas. Mechanism, sequence, mind map, loop. Produce it from nothing, then check it.

  • Brain dumps

    A prompt, a blank page, a timer. Two prompts for every competency.

  • The competency list

    All 268, in full, plus the anatomy, chemistry and math assumed coming in.
