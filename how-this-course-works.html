How this course works.

Every week is one page with the same seven stages in the same order. Learn the shape once, here, and you will never have to work out where to go next.

One path, several ways to walk it

The destination is fixed. The route is yours.

You do not choose

  • Where to go next, or what the week is about.
  • What the competencies are.
  • What is due, or when.
  • What you have to be able to do at the end.

You do choose

  • Which representation helps you learn: video, notes, textbook, drawing, out loud, or a combination.
  • How you retrieve it.
  • Which application case you solve, and which room it is set in.
  • When in the week you work.
  • What you change when something is not working.

A course with no choice ignores that you are all arriving from different places. A course that is nothing but choice hands you a menu and leaves you to guess what mattered. This one fixes the destination and opens up the route.

    What mastery means here

    If I tell you that you choose how you learn, I owe you a way to find out whether your choice worked.

    You have not mastered something because you watched it, read it, highlighted it, or recognized it when you saw it. You have mastered a competency when you can retrieve what you know without your resources and use it to solve a problem you have not seen before.

    Four levels, and only you can tell which one you are at

    1. I recognize it. I have seen this before. It looks familiar on the page.
    2. I can recall it. I can explain or reconstruct it without looking at anything.
    3. I can use it. I can apply it to a problem I have not seen before.
    4. I can reason with it. I can predict what happens when the system changes, and explain why.

    This course means levels 3 and 4, with recall as the foundation that makes either possible. Recognition is not on the list. It is the level that feels like understanding while you are reading and disappears the moment the notes are closed.

    The branch at stage four

    The Mastery Check is a gate between learning the material and using it.

    All competencies solid? Go to Investigate and Apply. Rereading material you can already use is the least valuable hour available to you.

    Not yet? You will be told exactly which competency, and given a different resource to try. Retrieve and practice that one thing, retake the check, then move on.

    Going round that loop twice is not falling behind. It is the course working the way it was built to work, and it costs far less than finding out at a midterm.
