HOW TO PUSH IT
1. Unzip this file. You get a folder called PUSH-BIO005-sep08.
2. Open the folder. Select everything INSIDE it (Command A), including the
   os, tools, assets and compliance folders. Do not drag the outer folder.
3. Go to github.com/drsrennie-stack/human-physiology-Fa26
4. Add file, then Upload files.
5. Drag the selection onto the page. Wait for the count to finish.
6. Commit changes.

Delete this READ-ME-FIRST.txt before you drag, or it lands in the repo.
