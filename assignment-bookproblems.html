What this is

The assigned Silverthorn problems for Week 1. These are the calculations and the predict-and-explain reasoning your midterms ask for, worked while the material is still fresh. The specific problem numbers are on this week's page.

Due Sunday at 10:00 pm, the Sunday that closes Week 1. Up to 24 hours late is an automatic 50 percent reduction. After 24 hours it is a zero.

How to work them

  1. Try each one before you look anything upGet as far as you can from what you know. The point where you get stuck is information: it tells you which competency has not landed yet.
  2. Show every stepFor any calculation, write the equation, then the substitution, then the answer with units. An answer on its own earns almost nothing here, because the reasoning is what I am reading.
  3. Say what the number meansAfter you calculate something, add one line on what it tells you physiologically. A number you cannot interpret is not yet useful to you.
  4. Mark what you had to look upPut a star beside any problem where you needed the book, the video or help. Those stars are your study list for the midterm.

Using AI on this

AI is open. The honest way to use it here is to work the problem yourself first, then ask AI to check your reasoning and tell you where it breaks. Asking for the answer first and writing it down teaches you nothing, and it shows up immediately on a midterm where you have to produce the reasoning out loud with no notes.
