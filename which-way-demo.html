What you are looking at, and what students would build

Everything in this game comes from one list sitting at the top of the file, marked THE PHYSIOLOGY. It is eight scenarios, each with the variables, the right direction for each, and the reason. That list is the part a student writes.

Everything under the second marker is the game. It has no physiology in it at all. It does not know what a sarcomere is. It just reads the list, asks, checks, and keeps score. That part is identical for every student in the section.

Add a ninth scenario to the list and it appears in the game. Nothing else has to change. That is the requirement, working.
