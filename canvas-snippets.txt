<h2>The five-minute practice round</h2>
<p>Pick one competency from this week. Blank paper, a pen, your phone camera, and no notes.</p>
<ol>
<li>Draw the mechanism from memory. Label it.</li>
<li>Start recording and teach it out loud, step by step, the way you would teach a classmate. Say why each step happens, not only what happens.</li>
<li>Stop at five minutes. Watch it back once. Anything you could not say without looking goes on your miss list for the recall cards.</li>
</ol>
<p>Nothing is turned in. This is the exact shape of the midterm, practiced while nothing is on the line.</p>
