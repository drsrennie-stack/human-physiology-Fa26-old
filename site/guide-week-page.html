How the week page works

Every week in this course looks the same. Learn the layout once and you will never have to wonder where anything is again.

About 4 minutes to read

One page per week, and it is the only page you need

The week page is the starting point for everything. It carries the reading, the video, the lab, the problem set, the chart entry and the post, in the order you should do them. If something is not on the week page, it is not required that week.

The steps are in order for a reason

They are not a menu. Each one sets up the next, and the last three assume the earlier ones actually happened.

The reading is more useful after the video, because the video tells you what to watch for. The practice questions are more useful after the reading. The chart entry only works if you have not just read the answer.

Doing the steps out of order is not against the rules. It just wastes most of the value.

Ticking things off

The checkboxes are for you. Nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is graded from them, and they live in your own browser. They exist because a fifteen week course is long and it helps to see where you stopped.

  • Progress is saved on the device you used, so it will not follow you to a different computer.
  • Clearing your browser data clears them.
  • Your actual grades live in Canvas, always.

The parts that are folded up

Three sections on each week page are collapsed by default, because they are references rather than instructions.

  • What to watch for, the specific things to keep an eye out for while you read.
  • Structures you should already know, a short list per week. You do not need to have taken anatomy for this course. You do need to look these up when they appear.
  • Competencies, the full list of what you should be able to do by the end of the week. Nothing on an exam comes from outside it.

Coming Friday

New material opens every Friday and that week's work is due the following Friday at 11:59 pm. If a button on the week page says a link is coming Friday, that is what it means: the page is built, the Canvas side opens on the drop day.

That cadence exists so you can start over the weekend while the material is fresh and finish during the week, and so you always know what day things are due without checking.
