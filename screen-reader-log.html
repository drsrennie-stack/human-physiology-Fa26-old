Start with a template, since one test covers every page built from it. Leave the labs and the Mastery OS until last, because they take the longest and they are where problems usually are.

Open this page in a new tab

1. Do the headings read as an outline?

Press VO + U to open the rotor, arrow across to Headings. One first level heading that says what the page is, then the sections under it, no levels skipped. This one check catches most structural problems.

2. Are the landmarks there?

Rotor again, arrow to Landmarks. You should hear banner, navigation, main and content information. That is what lets someone skip your navigation instead of hearing it on every page.

3. Do the link names make sense on their own?

Rotor, arrow to Links. They are heard with no surrounding sentence, so every name has to stand alone. Five links all called "read more" is a problem. "Back to Week 1" is not.

4. Can you tab all the way through without getting stuck?

Close the rotor with Esc, then Tab from the top with no mouse. Every stop should say what it is, what it does, and what state it is in, and you should always be able to get back out of anything you go into.

5. Does it say something when a value changes?

Only for the pages with a simulator, a tab set or a drawing canvas. Change something and listen. If the number on screen moves and nothing is announced, a blind student has no idea it happened.

The screen off run

Turn the brightness all the way down and do it once more. This is the real test. It is the one that tells you whether the page works or whether you were reading along with your eyes without noticing.
