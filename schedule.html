The term project: your patient file

One project runs the whole term. In Week 1 you are assigned a patient who is yours alone, with their own age, history, and baseline numbers. No two students have the same patient, so there is no answer to look up.

  1. Every week your patient meets one disturbance tied to that week's material.
  2. You draw the control loop that responds, by hand: sensor, integrating center, effector, the regulated variable, and which way the correction goes.
  3. You predict what the numbers do, and write one line on what breaks if the loop fails.
  4. You log where you used AI, what it got wrong for your specific patient, and how you corrected it.

Your patient also carries one condition you are not told about. It never shows up in a single week. It only appears as a pattern across several. On December 16 you assemble all fifteen loops, trace one disturbance across three systems on video, then name that condition and defend it using entries you wrote back in October.

These weekly entries are also your lab work. There is no proctored final exam.
