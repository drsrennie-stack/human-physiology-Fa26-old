Reading a Physiology Figure or Graph

Physiology is taught in pictures. Curves, flow diagrams, pressure traces. Most students skim past them and read only the words, then wonder why the exam questions feel unfamiliar. This week you slow down on one figure and find out what you actually see in it versus what you assumed.

Do the Learning Lab first

This post is built out of the lab. You cannot write it without having done the work, and that is on purpose. The lab walks you through four steps: pick your figure, first look, investigate, then recreate it from memory.
